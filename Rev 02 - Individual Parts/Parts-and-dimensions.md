# Trash Bin Pro Max
## Individual parts and dimensions · Rev 02

This cut list details the cabinet, four liners, fixed pedestal, deck blank, camera mast and electronics mounting parts. All dimensions are in **mm**. The **785 W × 760 D × 1,225 H** exterior envelope includes the LCD housing. The cabinet measures **760 × 760**.

The finished liner exterior is **346 × 346 × 385**. Its internal opening is **340 × 340 × 380** before the corner chamfer and pull loops. The previous drawing showed the **350 × 350** space available between the dividers and outer walls. The 346 mm body leaves 2 mm installation clearance per side, and its two 3 mm walls leave 340 mm inside. The inner corner uses a straight 45° chamfer for flat-panel construction.

### Datums and tolerances

- The cabinet center is X0, Y0. +X points toward the LCD and +Y toward the rear. Z0 is the floor under the feet.
- Z20 is the plinth underside, Z55 its top, Z115 the liner bottom, Z120 the liner floor surface, and Z500 the rim. The fixed pedestal ends at Z630. The deck top is intended for Z750 once its moving support is designed.
- Prototype cut targets are ±0.5 for cut lengths and profiles, ±0.5 for hole centers unless noted, and ±1 for the assembled liner's outside width and length. Measure stock thickness before cutting matched interfaces, square the cabinet before fixing the walls, and finish one trial liner before making the other three.
- Coordinates describe finished features. Dimensions listed to two places specify nominal geometry for the mitered parts and hole patterns.

### Assembly checks

1. Dry-fit the carved liner corner and check it slides past the pedestal, dividers and mast. Check the two liners on each removable-panel side independently.
2. Use the base as a drilling jig for the pedestal and the gussets as a drilling jig for the mast and arm. Verify that the camera points at X0, Y0 after tightening.
3. Check the dimensioned cover and sensor blanks against the actual electronics before cutting their module-specific openings. Deck and top plate attachment patterns depend on the later moving assembly.
4. The drawings specify the proposed prototype geometry. Check cabinet stiffness, level and access with a dry fit before treating the dimensions as a production release.
